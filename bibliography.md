This site's chapter arc, coverage, and level of rigor draw on a small set of
reference works. They are reference works, not sources: no text or code from any
of them is reproduced here. Each chapter's "For deeper coverage" section links the
relevant ones directly to the publisher.

| Title | Author(s) | Publisher | Year | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [*The Art of Software Testing*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Art+of+Software+Testing%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9781118031964) | Glenford Myers, Tom Badgett, Corey Sandler | Wiley | 2011 | Test-case design and boundary analysis |
| [*xUnit Test Patterns*](https://www.informit.com/store/xunit-test-patterns-refactoring-test-code-9780131495050) | Gerard Meszaros | Addison-Wesley | 2007 | Test doubles, fixtures, and test smells |
| [*Working Effectively with Legacy Code*](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/working-effectively-with-legacy-code/P200000009127) | Michael Feathers | Addison-Wesley | 2004 | Seams and dependency-breaking for testability |
| [*C++ Concurrency in Action*](https://www.manning.com/books/c-plus-plus-concurrency-in-action-second-edition) | Anthony Williams | Manning | 2019 | The C++ thread library and testing concurrent code |
| [*The Art of Debugging with GDB, DDD, and Eclipse*](https://nostarch.com/debugging.htm) | Norman Matloff, Peter Jay Salzman | No Starch Press | 2008 | A practical tour of GDB |
| [*Professional CMake: A Practical Guide*](https://crascit.com/professional-cmake/) | Craig Scott | Crascit | 2018 | CMake project structure, presets, and testing |
| [*Software Engineering at Google*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/software-engineering-at/9781492082781/) | Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck, Hyrum Wright (eds.) | O'Reilly | 2020 | Testing and continuous integration at scale |
